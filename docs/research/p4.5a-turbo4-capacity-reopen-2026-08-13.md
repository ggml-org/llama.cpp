## Decode guardrail (`bench.json`, sole tenancy, depth 16384, 7 alternating pairs/model)

turbo4 is not merely capacity-neutral here - it decodes measurably **faster** than q4_0 at
this depth on both models:

| Model | mean delta | sample stdev | lower 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

Both far clear the `>= -2%` reopen guardrail with room to spare. This is a positive result
beyond what the reopen condition required, not just a pass.

## Standing decision this does NOT change

Per `RALPH_PROMPT.md` and this repo's `CLAUDE.md`: **turbo remains a capacity feature, not
a speed feature.** This result validates turbo4 as a legitimate 2x KV-capacity mechanism
for dense low-GQA models with a PPL edge over q4_0 at the same capacity class, and shows no
decode-speed penalty at this depth - a stronger result than the bar required, but it is not
a general throughput claim and does not reopen the closed FA-speed-chase line of work. No
default changes are made by this task.

Full raw evidence is in the companion `p4.5a-turbo4-capacity-reopen-2026-08-13/`
directory: `harness.log` (the gate evidence), `short-*.log`/`full-*.log` (all 8 PPL
cells per model), `capacity-*.log`, `bench-*.jsonl`/`.fuser` (all warmup and 7-pair
decode legs), `manifest.json`, `build-metadata.json`, `sha256.txt`, `commands.txt`,
and dmesg before/after. `version-correctness.txt`/`version-llama-bench.txt` are
present but are build-identity probes, not gate evidence - see the note under "Gate
results" above.
