The receipts from the most recent Tessera waves. Each entry is a link to the receipt artifact, the kernel hash, and the per-tensor t_l² that the calibrator's fitness signal was measured against.

Coming soon

Living evidence is Phase 3.

The evidence page is the load-bearing surface of the Observatory: it is the live view of the system, not a stale snapshot. The bootstrap ships with the page in place but the live wiring is Phase 3 in the docs plan.

When Phase 3 lands, this page will be regenerated by the build:

  • per_tensor_calibrate on the canonical calibration corpus → fresh per-tensor stats
  • l5_orchestrator → fresh L5 plan summary
  • tessera-ab-harness → fresh A/B results
  • clip-capture → fresh multimodal capture stats

Until then, the receipts live in the repository and the changelog points to the most recent waves.
