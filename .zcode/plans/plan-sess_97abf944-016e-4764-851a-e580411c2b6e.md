### Part 2 - Wire L5 adaptive-requantize into dispatch (the substantive change)

#### Mapping decision (per your answer)

Two-stage tighten per flagged tensor, with the second stage gated by an A/B per family:

- **Stage A (multiplier on current values).** For each flagged tensor, re-quantize with
  - `alpha_new = clamp(alpha_current * spec.new_alpha, 0.0, 1.0)` (treating `new_alpha` as a multiplier, since L5 emits it in [0.1, 0.5])
  - `clip_new  = clamp(clip_current  * spec.new_clip,  0.7, 1.0)` (clip stays in valid range; if `alpha_current == 0` AWQ stays off)
  - everything else (`outlier_fraction`, `ternary_threshold`, `moment_mix`, etc.) unchanged from the GA/policy values.
- **Stage B (outlier_fraction A/B per family).** For each *semantic family* that has at least one flagged tensor (`attn_q`, `attn_k`, `attn_v`, `attn_out`, `ffn_gate`, `ffn_up`, `ffn_down` via `ts_regime_infer_family`), also evaluate a second candidate that instead raises `outlier_fraction` by a family-level multiplier (`outlier_frac * (1 + overshoot_scale * overshoot)`, capped). Pick whichever of {Stage A, Stage B} gives lower `relative_frobenius` for one representative tensor of that family; apply the winning strategy to all flagged tensors in that family. Record the per-family winner in the L5 report JSON.

The multiplier-on-current-values approach mirrors `ts_expert_profile.alpha_scale`/`clip_scale` already used by the regime router (`tessera-regime.h:64-65`), so it is consistent with an existing pattern rather than a new convention.

#### Scope (per your answer): full generational loop

New `--tessera-adaptive-requantize` flag. After step 7 of `ts_dispatch_run` (the per-tensor quantize loop, ends ~line 1349), insert the refine loop before step 8 (GGUF metadata/write). For up to `--tessera-l5-generations` (default 3):

1. **L2 measure.** Build a `std::vector<ts_l2_tensor_input>` over the refined tensors, pairing each tensor's source weights with its current `qr.recon`. Re-read source weights from the input GGUF on demand (per your memory answer) via the existing `ts_tensor_to_f32` path rather than retaining all sources in memory. Call `ts_l2_run` with `flag_multiplier` from params. qtype is `"tessera_t640"` (matches `ts_l5_ladder_index`).
2. **L5 plan.** `ts_l5_adaptive_requant(&l2_report, &l5_params, gen, &plan)`.
3. **Terminate** if `plan.n_requant == 0` or the plan is empty.
4. **A/B per family + re-quantize flagged tensors.** Group plan specs by `ts_regime_infer_family(spec.tensor_name)`. For each family: re-quantize one representative tensor under Stage A (alpha/clip multiplier) and under Stage B (outlier_fraction bump), measure `relative_frobenius` of each, pick the winner, apply it to every flagged tensor in that family. Re-quantization reuses `ts_quantize_2d` / `ts_quantize_3d` with the tightened `ts_quant_params_2d`, overwriting the existing `ts_quant_result_2d` in `cluster_results`/`moe_results` in place. Since the GGUF write at step 9 hasn't happened yet, the refined buffers flow into `ts_gguf_write_tensor_cluster` naturally.

The loop emits an L2/L5 report JSON (schema `llama.tessera.l5-loop.v1`) at `--tessera-l5-out` (default: beside `policy_out_path`), recording per-generation: n_flagged, n_requant, per-family winning strategy, before/after `relative_frobenius` per flagged tensor.

### Risk notes

- **In-place re-quantize correctness.** Whether overwriting the `ts_quant_result_2d` in the deque is enough for `ts_gguf_write_tensor_cluster` to pick up the new buffers depends on whether the writer copies or stores pointers at step 7. I will confirm this in `tessera-gguf-writer.cpp` at the start of implementation; if it copies, the refine loop will re-call `ts_gguf_write_tensor_cluster` for each refined tensor (cheap) rather than relying on in-place mutation. The plan above already accounts for both.
- **Re-reading source weights.** Adds one GGUF read per flagged tensor per generation. For typical runs (small minority of tensors flagged, <=3 generations) this is negligible I/O versus the original quantize pass.
- **A/B cost.** Two `ts_quantize_2d` calls per family per generation (one for Stage A, one for Stage B), not per tensor. With ~7 families this is at most 14 extra quantize calls per generation - small relative to the full-model pass.
